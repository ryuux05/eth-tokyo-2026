# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Technical hackathon operators configuring one agent account and its onchain execution policy. They have a human wallet and an operating signing key, and need to see exactly what will be deployed and what authority the agent receives.

## Product Purpose

Agentic World gives an agent its own persistent identity and authentication key. The owner portal creates or verifies that account, manages the operating key and onchain execution policy, and makes the service-authentication boundary clear. Success means an operator can complete setup without handling an agent-root EOA key or confusing account execution policy with service resource permissions.

## Brand Personality

Precise, capable, and calm. The interface should feel like a trustworthy protocol workbench: technically honest, quick to scan, and distinct without ornamental complexity.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboard cards, faux-glass crypto styling, dense wallet jargon without explanation, and decorative graphics that obscure the setup sequence.

## Design Principles

1. Show the current chain, account, owner, operating key, and policy state before any transaction.
2. Make the next safe action obvious; distinguish read-only checks from wallet transactions.
3. Keep identity, onchain execution policy, and service authorization visibly separate.
4. Expose exact addresses and transaction outcomes for technical verification without overwhelming the primary flow.
5. Explain failure states with a corrective next step.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Responsive layout, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus states, readable contrast, status announcements, and reduced-motion support. Do not rely on color alone to communicate safety or completion.
